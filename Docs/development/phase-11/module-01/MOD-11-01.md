## 1. Module Objective

Let several people contribute to one archive with clear roles, and let a group assemble one memory together.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Blueprint §7.2 V1.1: "the family archive is inherently multi-player; drives organic growth".
- §7.2 V1.2 calls collaborative capsules "the strongest acquisition loop in the whole concept" — one surprise-video project brings fifteen contacts into the product.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Shared vaults with owner, admin, contributor, and viewer roles.
- Invitations with scoped permissions.
- Shared albums with activity.
- Collaborative capsules: invite contributors, track submissions, auto-stitch, then schedule.
- Contributor web capture requiring no account (`/j/[token]`).
- Comments and reactions within shared vaults.
