## 1. Module Objective

Enable festival broadcasts with real personalisation and real abuse controls, and complete the channel cascade.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Broadcasts are emotionally valuable and viral, and they are also the highest abuse risk in the product (§29.18).
- The multi-channel cascade is the literal §14 promise and the answer to stale contact details over long horizons.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Festival packs with region-aware, lunar, and movable-feast date rules.
- Broadcast composer with merge tags and per-recipient preview.
- Audience selection with a recipient count.
- Post-send report with retry-failed.
- WhatsApp Business API and SMS channels added to the PHASE-05 ladder.
- Backup guardians for long-horizon capsules.
- Milestone-based delivery conditions.

## 9. Security Requirements

- **Mandatory abuse controls (§29.18):** per-tier recipient caps, per-day broadcast caps, timezone-aware quiet hours, and a persistent per-sender recipient opt-out that survives every future broadcast.
- No broadcasts to members who have never engaged.
