## 1. Module Objective

Ship the AI features in the order blueprint §15.3 specifies, with cost guardrails from day one.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Blueprint §15 ranks semantic search highest: "it is the explicit promise, it is used repeatedly, and it gets better as the archive grows — which is exactly the retention dynamic the product wants".
- §15 A3 notes transcription is "very high and under-appreciated": the most precious content in a family archive is a voice telling a story, and it is unsearchable everywhere today.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Semantic search over captions and transcripts, hybrid-fused with keyword results.
- Voice and video transcription, which also supplies accessibility captions.
- Auto event grouping — **deterministic, not AI** (§15 A6).
- Auto recaps: a slideshow version first, cinematic later.
- Suggested capsules (§15 A10) and AI memory prompts.
- Auto-captioning as the substrate for search.

## 7. Database Requirements

---

## 8. Integration Requirements

- Claude for narrative and curation copy
- Whisper-class ASR
- Embeddings provider

---

## 9. Security Requirements

- **AI processing is opt-in and per-purpose** (§11.6), with a single global switch that genuinely disables captioning, transcription, and embeddings.
- A contractual commitment that user content is not used to train third-party models.
- **Face recognition is not built** in this phase or any phase in this plan, pending legal review.

---

## 10. Testing Requirements

- Retrieval quality
- Transcription accuracy sampling
- Grouping correctness
- Cost-budget enforcement
- Opt-out completeness
