## 1. Module Objective

Make an interrupted upload an inconvenience rather than a loss, and make the queue visible and controllable.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- A lost upload is the failure most likely to lose a user permanently, because it destroys the premise.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Server-side upload sessions with part manifests.
- Presigned multipart URLs, three-way parallel PUTs, resume from the last confirmed part.
- the on-device store outbox with backoff and idempotency.
- Queue UI with pause, resume, cancel, retry.
- Quota checked before any bytes move.

## 9. Security Requirements

- Magic-byte validation
- SVG rejected
- Per-tier size caps
- Quota enforced server-side before presigning
