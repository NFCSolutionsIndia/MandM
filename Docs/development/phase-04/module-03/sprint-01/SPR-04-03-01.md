## 1. Sprint Goal

An uploaded photo shows a blurhash instantly, a thumbnail in the grid, and a preview in detail; an uploaded video streams adaptively; and none of it is reachable without a fresh signed URL.

---

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Image variants and blurhash
- Video HLS and poster
- Audio normalisation and waveform
- EXIF with GPS stripping
- Signed-URL endpoint
- Media components

### Out of Scope

- Perceptual hashing and dedupe (Release 2)
- Recap rendering (Release 2)
- AI captioning (Release 2)

---

## 3. Technical Approach

- Run processing in an isolated container with no database access, receiving work through the job payload and writing results back through a narrow, authenticated callback — FFmpeg on untrusted input is treated as hostile (§20.4).
- Originals are read, never written.

---

## 4. Architecture Changes

- Introduces the media-processing worker and the signed-URL delivery path
