## 1. Module Objective

Make media load quickly on a poor connection while keeping originals untouched and every byte behind an authorization check.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Perceived performance is most of the felt quality of a media product, and egress is most of its cost.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Thumbnail, preview, and blurhash generation.
- Video HLS ladder with a poster frame.
- Audio normalisation with a waveform.
- EXIF extraction with GPS stripped by default.
- Signed-URL minting with rate limits.
- Progressive image and adaptive video components.

---

_Not applicable to this module._

_Not applicable — this module has no user-facing surface._

---

## 6. Backend Requirements

- FFmpeg and sharp in an isolated container
- Job handlers on the PHASE-01 registry
- Signed-URL endpoint

---

## 7. Database Requirements

- Updates `media_assets` variant fields and `transcode_status`

---

## 8. Integration Requirements

_No external integrations._

---

## 9. Security Requirements

- Processing container: non-root, no database credentials, no egress beyond storage (§20.4).
- Signed URLs only; five-minute TTL; per-user minting limits.
- GPS stripped unless opted in.

---

## 10. Testing Requirements

- Corrupt and oversized inputs
- Rotation and HDR correctness
- Expired-URL behaviour
- Cross-user denial
