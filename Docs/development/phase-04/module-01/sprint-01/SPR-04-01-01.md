## 1. Sprint Goal

A user records video, voice, or photos in the app; the result is in the on-device store before any network request is made.

---

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Video capture
- Voice capture with waveform
- Photo selection with reorder
- Codec negotiation
- the on-device store blob store
- Permission primers
- Wake lock

### Out of Scope

- Upload (next module)
- Editing, filters, music (Release 2)
- Transcoding (MOD-04-03)

---

## 3. Technical Approach

- Detect and negotiate codecs explicitly rather than assuming; Chromium prefers VP9/WebM/Opus and Safari H.264/MP4/AAC.
- Write to the on-device store first, always. Treat the blob as the memory from that moment.
- Keep the `<input capture>` fallback fully functional — it is the safety net on any engine where `MediaRecorder` misbehaves.

---

## 4. Architecture Changes

- Introduces the local blob store that PHASE-06 extends into the full offline outbox
