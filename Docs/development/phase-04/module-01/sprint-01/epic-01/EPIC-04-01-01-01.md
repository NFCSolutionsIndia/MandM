## 1. Epic Objective

Build video, voice, and photo capture that works across engines and never loses a completed recording.

---

## 2. Business Value

A voice note from a grandparent is the single most valuable artifact this product can hold. Losing one to a codec assumption is unacceptable.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to record a message without leaving the app
> **So that** the moment does not pass while I hunt for a camera app

> **As a** grandparent
> **I want** to record my voice telling a story
> **So that** my grandchildren can hear it, not just read about it

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Video with flip, flash, timer, and plan-based duration ring
- Voice with live waveform, pause/resume, and trim handles
- Photo multi-select up to 20 with drag reorder
- Universal file fallback

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Explicit `MediaRecorder.isTypeSupported` negotiation with a documented fallback chain
- Screen Wake Lock during recording
- Blob written to the on-device store before the recorder is torn down

---
