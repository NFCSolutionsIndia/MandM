## 1. Module Objective

Remove the gap between intention and artifact — and make the recorded blob durable before anything else happens.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Blueprint §2.1: "the emotional payload is a voice or a face, not a JPEG". Upload-only would make the MVP a file manager.
- §29.14 requires capture with no editing step: "No complex editing required" is a stated product feature.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Photo, video, and voice capture with a live preview and duration limits by plan.
- Universal fallback via `<input type="file" capture>`.
- Permission primer before the browser prompt.
- The recorded blob persists locally before any network call.

---

## 4. User Flows

_Not applicable to this module._

---

## 5. UI/UX Requirements

- Per §29.14: camera with flip, timer, ring progress; voice with live waveform; photo multi-select with reorder

---

## 6. Backend Requirements

- None — capture is entirely client-side until the upload session is requested

_No schema changes owned by this module._

## 8. Integration Requirements

_No external integrations._

---

## 9. Security Requirements

- Camera and microphone permission explained before requesting
- Blob stored in the on-device store, not in memory

---

## 10. Testing Requirements

- Codec fallback across engines
- Permission denial recovery
- Blob survives a forced reload
