## 1. Epic Objective

Build revocable share links and make access visible to the owner.

---

## 2. Business Value

Blueprint §24.1 lists "make privacy visible rather than merely claimed" as a UX improvement over the existing product: "Demonstrated control beats claimed encryption."

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to send one photo to my mother without her signing up
> **So that** sharing is not a barrier

> **As a** user
> **I want** to see every active link and kill any of them
> **So that** I know exactly who can reach my archive

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Create link with options
- Public view
- Revoke
- View count and last viewed
- First-view notification
- Active-links list

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Hashed CSPRNG tokens
- Signed media minted per request after authz
- Revocation effective immediately

---

## 6. UI Requirements

- Share sheet
- `/s/[token]`
- Who-can-see panel
