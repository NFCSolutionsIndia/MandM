## 1. Module Objective

Let a user share one memory without exposing anything else, and take it back instantly.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Blueprint §7.1 M11: "Family will not sign up to see a photo." Without this the archive is a dead end.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Create a link with permission, expiry, password, and download flag
- Public view without an account
- Instant revocation
- Who-can-see panel with active links and view counts

---

_Not applicable to this module._
