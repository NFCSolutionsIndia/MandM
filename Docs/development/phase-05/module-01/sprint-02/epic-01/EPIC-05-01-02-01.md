## 1. Epic Objective

Build the seal screen and the sender-side capsule detail.

---

## 2. Business Value

Blueprint §29.15 calls the seal screen "the most important screen in the product. Turn a memory into a promise." The detail screen is what keeps that promise credible across the years of silence that follow.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** parent
> **I want** to understand exactly what will happen before I commit
> **So that** I can trust a promise I am making for eighteen years

> **As a** user years later
> **I want** to confirm my capsule is still safe and on track
> **So that** I do not quietly stop believing in it

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Recipient picker
- Unlock condition selector
- Channel preview with reasons
- Summary block
- Seal confirmation
- Detail with countdown, status history, edit, cancel
- Contact freshness prompt

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Both timezones displayed
- Gate errors mapped to specific copy
- Status history from the attempt log
