## 1. Epic Objective

Create the capsule entities and a seal operation that cannot leave an inconsistent state.

---

## 2. Business Value

A capsule that is sealed but not scheduled is a broken promise that nobody discovers until the date passes. Atomicity is what makes the guarantee real rather than aspirational.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** parent
> **I want** to address a video to my daughter for her eighteenth birthday
> **So that** I can speak to her in 2044

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Capsule CRUD with state machine
- Recipients with contact snapshots
- Seal transaction
- Immutability
- Cancellation
- Entitlement gates

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- `(state, unlock_at)` index as the scheduler hot path
- Tokens hashed and versioned
- Entitlements snapshotted at seal
