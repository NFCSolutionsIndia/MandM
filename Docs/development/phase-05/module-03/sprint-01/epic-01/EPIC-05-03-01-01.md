## 1. Epic Objective

Build the unlock page and the reply loop.

---

## 2. Business Value

Blueprint §29.24 calls this "the most emotionally important screen in the product". §24.2 identifies the reply as what converts a one-way broadcast into a relationship — and a recipient into a user.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** recipient
> **I want** to open what was left for me without signing up for anything
> **So that** nothing stands between me and the message

> **As a** recipient
> **I want** to say something back
> **So that** the person who made this knows it reached me

> **As a** sender
> **I want** to know it was opened
> **So that** the promise completed

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Sealed, delivered, expired, revoked, and not-found states
- Slow reveal
- Full-screen media
- Reply
- Save a copy
- Soft invitation

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Server components only
- Progressive enhancement
- Media preloaded before the reveal so it never stutters
