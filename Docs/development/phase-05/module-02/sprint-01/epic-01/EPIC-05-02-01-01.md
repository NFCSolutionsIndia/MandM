## 1. Epic Objective

Build the claim loop, the fan-out, and the email channel with a decade-durable template.

---

## 2. Business Value

This is the machinery behind the product's only unique promise.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** recipient in 2044
> **I want** to receive what my father recorded for me
> **So that** the promise made in 2026 is kept

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Claim due jobs with locking
- Fan out per recipient and channel
- Push then email ladder
- Humane window at fire time
- Sender receipt
- Idempotent attempts

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`
- Database time only
- Resend with SES fallback
- Table-based email with plain-text alternative
