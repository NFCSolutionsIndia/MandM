## 1. Epic Objective

Create the member model and the screens to manage it.

---

## 2. Business Value

Members are the addressing layer for the product's differentiating feature. A capsule cannot exist without one.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to record the people who matter with their birthdays
> **So that** the app can remind me and I can address memories to them

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Full member CRUD
- Relationship chips with custom values
- Next-occasion subtext
- Undeliverable warning

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Every read and write through `lib/authz`
- Trigram index for later duplicate detection
- Birthday precision handled like memory dates

---
