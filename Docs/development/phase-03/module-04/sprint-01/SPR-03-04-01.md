## 1. Sprint Goal

A user can scroll their whole archive, jump to a year, filter it, search it, and be shown something from a past year without asking.

---

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Timeline query and virtualised view
- Scrubber and deep links
- Future band with capsule stubs
- Search screen
- Filters and favourites
- On This Day and Home cards

### Out of Scope

- Semantic search (Release 2)
- Real capsules in the future band (PHASE-05)
- Auto event grouping (Release 2)

---

## 3. Technical Approach

- Build the future band now with a documented stub interface, so PHASE-05 populates an existing surface rather than adding one.
- Treat the 2,000-item benchmark as an acceptance criterion measured on a throttled mid-range profile.

---

## 4. Architecture Changes

- Establishes the cursor-pagination pattern reused by gallery and search
