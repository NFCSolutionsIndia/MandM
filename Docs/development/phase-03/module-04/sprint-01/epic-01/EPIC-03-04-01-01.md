## 1. Epic Objective

Build the virtualised timeline with the Now divider and the future band.

---

## 2. Business Value

Blueprint §29.12: "the sharpest product insight in the repository. A timeline extending *forward* — pending capsules shown alongside past memories — is a genuinely novel primary navigation surface."

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to see my life laid out including what is still coming
> **So that** the archive feels alive rather than finished

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Cursor-paginated ranges
- Sticky scrubber with year jump
- Now divider
- Future band
- Density toggle
- Filter sheet
- Deep links

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Virtualised windowed list with stable heights
- Buffered adjacent ranges
- Offline ranges served from cache and marked

---

## 6. UI Requirements
