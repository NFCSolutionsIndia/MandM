## 1. Epic Objective

Build text search, shared filters, favourites, and the On This Day resurfacing that drives return visits.

---

## 2. Business Value

Blueprint §7.1 rates On This Day as "the cheapest, strongest retention mechanic in the product" — and notes it needs no AI, only dates.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to find a memory by typing a word
> **So that** I do not have to scroll years to find one photograph

> **As a** user
> **I want** to be shown something from a past year without asking
> **So that** the archive surprises me instead of waiting

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Search across memories, members, events
- Suggestion chips and recent searches
- Shared filters
- Favourites
- On This Day card and weekly push

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- a tsvector GIN index, no trigram fallback until R2 (ADR-010)
- Precision-aware On This Day matching
- Weekly cap on the notification

---

## 6. UI Requirements
