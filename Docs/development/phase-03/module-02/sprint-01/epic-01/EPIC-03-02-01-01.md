## 1. Epic Objective

Create the event schema and correct occurrence computation.

---

## 2. Business Value

Every prompt, reminder, and future-facing surface in the product depends on knowing when the next occurrence of something is.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to add my mother's birthday once and be reminded every year
> **So that** I never miss it again

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Event CRUD with recurrence
- Member linking, many-to-many
- Occurrence computation for a date range
- Auto-derived birthdays from members

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Occurrences computed, never materialised
- Absolute-instant arithmetic with explicit timezone
- Leap-day and DST correctness

---
