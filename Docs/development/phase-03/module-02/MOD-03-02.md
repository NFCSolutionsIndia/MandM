## 1. Module Objective

Turn a passive archive into a product that prompts action, by knowing what is coming and reminding the user in time to do something about it.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Blueprint §27.3 identifies this as the most valuable addition the designer brief made, and §27.4 warns that retrofitting it is a migration rather than a feature.
- It is the shared trigger for memories now, and for broadcasts and gifts in later releases.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Create events with type, date, recurrence, and reminder offsets.
- Link events to one or more members.
- Auto-derive birthdays and anniversaries from member records.
- Month, year, and agenda views.
- Region-aware festival display.
- Reminder jobs fire once per occurrence.

---

## 4. User Flows

_Not applicable to this module._

---

## 5. UI/UX Requirements

- Three calendar views with a Today control (§29.12)
- Event chips by type with a recurrence indicator
- Event detail sheet with countdown ring and planned-items checklist

---

## 6. Backend Requirements

- Recurrence computation without materialising rows
- Reminder scheduling into the PHASE-01 job table
- Festival rules by region

---

## 7. Database Requirements

- `events`, `event_members`, `event_reminders` scoped through `lib/authz`
- Derived occurrences computed, not stored

---

## 8. Integration Requirements

_No external integrations._

---

## 9. Security Requirements

- Owner-scoped through `lib/authz`

---

## 10. Testing Requirements

- Recurrence across DST and leap years
- Reminder fired exactly once per year
- Timezone correctness
