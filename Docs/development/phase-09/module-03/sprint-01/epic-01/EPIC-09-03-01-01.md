## 1. Epic Objective

Verify the recovery and delivery-guarantee claims by executing the failures.

---

## 2. Business Value

This epic closes gap R-1. Everything the delivery design promises is asserted here by breaking it deliberately.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** operator
> **I want** to know we can restore from backup
> **So that** a storage incident is recoverable rather than terminal

> **As a** user
> **I want** confidence that a failed delivery is noticed
> **So that** the promise is monitored, not merely made

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Load test on upload and signed-URL minting
- Full restore into a clean environment
- Injection: lost job row, crashed worker, corrupted checksum, provider outage, missed unlock
- Runbooks

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Restore from actual backups without shortcuts
- Injection in staging with the real watchdog and sweep running
