## 1. Module Objective

Cover the critical path end to end, meet WCAG 2.2 AA in practice rather than on paper, and hold the performance baseline.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Grandparents are core users (§26.8). A product they cannot use has lost a third of its audience and its most emotionally valuable content.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Critical-path E2E
- Offline and install E2E
- Accessibility audit with a screen reader
- Device lab
- Performance regression gate
