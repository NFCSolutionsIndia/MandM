## 1. Epic Objective

Create the identity tables and a session service whose revocation is instant.

---

## 2. Business Value

A stolen or stale session on a product holding family video must be killable in one tap. Stateless tokens cannot offer that; rows can.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user whose phone was stolen
> **I want** to sign that phone out from another device immediately
> **So that** my memories are not sitting unlocked in someone else's pocket

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Create a user with a case-insensitive unique email.
- Issue a session bound to a device fingerprint and user agent.
- Validate a session on every request, sliding its expiry.
- List active sessions with device metadata; revoke one or all.

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Session token: 32 bytes CSPRNG, stored as SHA-256, compared in constant time.
- 30-day rolling expiry with sliding refresh (§20.1).
- Revocation is a row update; the next validation fails.

## 8. Database Requirements

- `users`, `sessions` scoped through `lib/authz`
- Case-insensitive unique email via `ICU collation`
- Index on `sessions.token_hash` and `sessions.user_id`

---

## 9. Security Requirements

- HTTP-only, Secure, SameSite=Lax cookies
- No PII in the cookie
- Constant-time token comparison

---

## 10. Testing Requirements

- Session lifecycle
- Revocation immediacy
- Sliding expiry
- tenant isolation on auth tables
