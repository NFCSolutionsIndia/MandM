## 1. Epic Objective

Give users fast, durable sign-in — and a way back in when a factor is lost.

---

## 2. Business Value

Passkeys remove the most common support burden and the most common breach vector. The recovery path is what makes a passwordless lifetime vault trustworthy rather than terrifying.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** returning user
> **I want** to sign in with my face
> **So that** getting into my own archive takes a second

> **As a** user who replaced their phone
> **I want** to get back in without losing everything
> **So that** a lost device is an inconvenience rather than a catastrophe

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Register a passkey; label it; remove it.
- Sign in with a passkey.
- Sign in with Google or Apple, including private-relay addresses.
- Recover access when the primary factor is lost.

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- WebAuthn registration and assertion with challenge verification and replay rejection.
- OAuth identity linked by provider account id, never by email.
- Recovery codes generated once, shown once, stored hashed.

## 9. Security Requirements

- Sign-count regression rejected as a cloning signal.
- OAuth email collision must not permit account takeover.
- Recovery must not create a social-engineering path — the policy is written, not improvised by support.

---

## 10. Testing Requirements

- Virtual authenticator register and login
- Replay rejection
- OAuth collision handling
- Recovery flow
