## 1. Sprint Goal

A person can sign up on their phone, verify, sign in later with a passkey, and recover access if they lose it.

---

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Signup, login, verification, reset screens
- Passkey registration and login
- Google and Apple OAuth
- Device list and revoke UI
- Account recovery policy and flow

### Out of Scope

- Onboarding quiz (MOD-02-03)
- Vault and authz (MOD-02-02)

---

## 3. Technical Approach

- Passkey-first for returning users, OTP as the universal fallback, OAuth as a convenience.
- Offer passkey creation immediately after signup and again after a successful reset, since the user has just proven control.
- Every screen uses the PHASE-01 form primitives so the §33 validation timing is inherited rather than reimplemented.

---

## 4. Architecture Changes

- Introduces the WebAuthn ceremony endpoints and the OAuth callback surface
