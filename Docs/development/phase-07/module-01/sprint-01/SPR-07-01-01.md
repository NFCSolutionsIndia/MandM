## 1. Sprint Goal

A notification can be enqueued by any feature and reaches the user through the right channels, is recorded in-app, and respects their preferences.

---

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- APNs/FCM credentials and device lifecycle
- Native push handlers
- Permission timing
- Notification entity and dispatch
- Centre screen
- Preferences and quiet hours

### Out of Scope

- The trigger matrix itself (next module)
- SMS and WhatsApp (Release 2)

---

## 3. Technical Approach

- One dispatch function every feature calls; channel selection, preference filtering, and quiet-hours deferral all live inside it, so no feature can accidentally bypass the rules.
- Email is unconditional for critical types regardless of push state (§14.2).

---

## 4. Architecture Changes

- Introduces the dispatch layer that every notification in the product flows through
