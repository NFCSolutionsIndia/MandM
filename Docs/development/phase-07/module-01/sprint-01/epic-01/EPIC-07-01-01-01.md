## 1. Epic Objective

Build push subscription, the background-sync handler, and the single dispatch function every feature uses.

---

## 2. Business Value

A single dispatch chokepoint is what makes the "be quiet" rule enforceable rather than aspirational.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to know the instant my capsule is opened
> **So that** the promise visibly completes

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Subscribe and store devices
- Prune dead endpoints
- SW handler with actions and deep links
- Contextual permission request at first seal
- Dispatch with channel selection

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- APNs/FCM credentials keys; encrypted payloads
- Idempotent enqueue by natural key
- Email unconditional for critical types

---
