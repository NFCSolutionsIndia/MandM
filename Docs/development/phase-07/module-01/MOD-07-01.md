## 1. Module Objective

Deliver notifications reliably across channels and keep a complete in-app record of everything sent.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- The delivery and open notifications are the product's emotional payoff and its growth loop.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Subscribe, refresh, and prune push devices
- Native push handlers with actions and deep links
- Contextual permission request
- Notification centre with grouping
- Preferences and quiet hours

---

_Not applicable to this module._

## 6. Backend Requirements

- APNs/FCM credentials push send
- Device lifecycle
- Notification entity and dispatch layer

## 7. Database Requirements

- `devices`, `notifications` scoped through `lib/authz`

## 8. Integration Requirements

- Non-suppressible categories enforced server-side
