## 1. Epic Objective

Implement every §14 Release-1 trigger and its email template, and measure the resulting volume.

---

## 2. Business Value

Blueprint §14 sets a numeric target for quietness. Implementing the matrix as data and measuring the result is what turns that target into a property of the system.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to be told when something actually matters
> **So that** I keep the app installed

> **As a** sender
> **I want** to know the moment my capsule is opened
> **So that** the promise completes

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Triggers: capsule delivered, capsule opened, reply received, delivery failed, delivering in 7 days, On This Day, upload finished, storage 90%/100%, new-device sign-in, share first viewed, credential change, payment failed.
- Email template per trigger.
- Bounce and complaint handling updating attempt state.
- Volume metric with alert.

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Each trigger is a registry entry plus a call site or job kind
- Once-only guaranteed by natural key
- Templates share a minimal, durable base
