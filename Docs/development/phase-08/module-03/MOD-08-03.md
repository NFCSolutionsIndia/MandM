## 1. Module Objective

Implement the §17.2 pricing model exactly, with entitlements that cannot retroactively invalidate a sealed capsule.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Media costs money from the first user. Blueprint §17.2 also decided that memories are never deleted and capsules never cancelled for non-payment — those constraints shape the implementation.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Subscription entity with snapshot entitlements
- Stripe and Razorpay checkout and portal
- Webhooks with idempotency
- Quota enforcement at upload
- Contextual paywalls
- Accounting reconciliation
