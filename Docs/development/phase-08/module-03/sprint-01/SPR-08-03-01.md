## 1. Sprint Goal

A user can subscribe with a card or UPI, their entitlements apply immediately, and nothing about billing can break an existing promise.

---

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Subscription schema and entitlements
- Stripe and Razorpay checkout and portal
- Webhooks
- Quota enforcement
- Paywalls
- Reconciliation

### Out of Scope

- Family plan (Release 2)
- Group gifting payments (Release 3)

---

## 3. Technical Approach

- Entitlements are denormalised onto the subscription row and snapshotted at seal time, exactly as §10 specifies, so a single read answers every gate.
