## 1. Epic Objective

Implement the decided pricing model with Stripe and Razorpay, and enforce entitlements without retroactive effect.

---

## 2. Business Value

Blueprint §17.2 item 4 makes one rule non-negotiable: "a sealed capsule is never cancelled for non-payment... This costs a little and is the single most trust-building line in the entire pricing model."

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user near my storage limit
> **I want** to upgrade in a few taps
> **So that** I can keep going without friction

> **As a** user whose card expired
> **I want** my sealed capsules to still arrive
> **So that** a billing problem does not break a promise I made to my daughter

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Free and Premium tiers per §17.2
- Stripe and Razorpay checkout
- Billing portal
- Webhooks
- Quota enforcement
- Four paywall triggers
- Dunning

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Entitlements denormalised on the subscription row
- Snapshot at seal time
- Webhook idempotency by event id
