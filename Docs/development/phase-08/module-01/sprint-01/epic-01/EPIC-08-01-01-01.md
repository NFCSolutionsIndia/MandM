## 1. Epic Objective

Record every content access and surface the user's own log to them.

---

## 2. Business Value

Blueprint §20.11 requires it for security; §11.5 and §24.1 turn it into a product feature. The same table serves both.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to see who has opened my memories and when
> **So that** privacy is something I can verify rather than something I am told

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Audit entries for content access, permission changes, share creation and revocation, capsule transitions, delivery attempts, admin actions, auth events
- User-visible log at country granularity
- 24-month retention

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Append-only in practice
- Written from the authz layer where possible
- IP hashed, never stored raw

---

_Not applicable._

## 8. Database Requirements

- `audit_log` scoped through `lib/authz` allowing a user to read their own entries

---

## 9. Security Requirements

- Admin content access always logged and always notified

---

## 10. Testing Requirements
