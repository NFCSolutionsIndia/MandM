## 1. Module Objective

Give the product a report path, a takedown workflow, and an operator console — without opening a door into private content.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Blueprint §27.4: "A media service without a report path and a takedown workflow is a legal exposure from its first public user."

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Report and block
- CSAM hash-matching on upload
- Moderation queue with SLA
- Admin auth realm with hardware MFA
- Delivery health and job queue
- User lookup with break-glass

---

## 8. Integration Requirements

_No external integrations._

---

## 9. Security Requirements

- Admin on a separate subdomain with hardware MFA and independent rate limits.
- Support sees no content by default; break-glass needs a reason, a second approver, and notifies the user.
- Only shared, reported, or auto-flagged content is reviewable.

---

## 10. Testing Requirements

- Break-glass enforcement
- Moderation boundary
- SLA timers
- Delivery alert wiring
