## 1. Sprint Goal

A user can report content and block a person; an operator sees it in a queue with an SLA and can act, with everything audited.

---

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Report and block
- CSAM hash-matching on upload
- Moderation queue with SLA
- Appeals

### Out of Scope

- Full classifier scoring (Release 2)
- Broadcast abuse tooling (Release 2)

---

## 3. Technical Approach

- Enforce the moderation boundary architecturally: the queue can only reach content that was shared, reported, or auto-flagged.

---

## 4. Architecture Changes

- Introduces the moderation boundary and the CSAM matching step in the upload pipeline
