## 1. Epic Objective

Build the duty-of-care path with a boundary that protects private content.

---

## 2. Business Value

This is a legal requirement and a moral one. It is also where a product can easily overreach — so the boundary matters as much as the capability.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to report something and block someone
> **So that** I have recourse

> **As a** operator
> **I want** to review a report quickly with a clear policy
> **So that** decisions are consistent and timely

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Report any shared resource
- Block a user or member
- CSAM hash-matching on upload
- Queue with SLA timers and actions
- Appeals tab

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Hash matching in the upload pipeline
- Queue reachable only for shared, reported, or flagged content
- SLA timer per item
