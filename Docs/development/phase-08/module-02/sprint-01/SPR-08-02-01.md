## 1. Sprint Goal

A user can download everything they have ever put in, and can delete their account with full knowledge of the consequences.

---

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Export job and download
- Deletion flow with capsule warning
- Grace period and recovery
- Purge handler

### Out of Scope

- Admin-initiated GDPR deletion (MOD-08-04)

---

## 3. Technical Approach

- Export streams rather than buffering, because a mature vault is hundreds of gigabytes.
