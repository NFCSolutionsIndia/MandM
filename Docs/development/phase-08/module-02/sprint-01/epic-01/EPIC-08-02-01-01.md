## 1. Epic Objective

Build complete export and a deletion flow that tells the truth about its consequences.

---

## 2. Business Value

Blueprint §20.9 frames both as trust features. The deletion warning about pending capsules is the part that matters most: deleting an account cancels promises made to other people, and that must never be a surprise.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** user
> **I want** to download everything I have put in
> **So that** I know I am not locked in

> **As a** user deleting my account
> **I want** to be told what promises that breaks
> **So that** I am not surprised by a consequence I could not have known

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Export originals plus manifest
- Signed 7-day download
- Two-step deletion with typed confirmation
- Pending-capsule warning by recipient name
- 30-day grace with recovery
- Complete purge

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Streaming ZIP
- Step-up auth for both
- Storage-first purge ordering
- Stripe subscription cancelled on deletion
