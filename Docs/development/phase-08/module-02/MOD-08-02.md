## 1. Module Objective

Make export and deletion complete, honest, and legally sufficient.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

- Blueprint §20.9: export is "a trust feature, not a compliance chore". Free full export is also a differentiator against services that charge an exit tax.

---

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Full export of originals plus a metadata manifest
- Two-step deletion with capsule warning
- 30-day grace period
- Complete purge with audit
