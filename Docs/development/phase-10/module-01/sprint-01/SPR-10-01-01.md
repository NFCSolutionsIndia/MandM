## 3. Technical Approach

- Reuse the existing repository's strongest copy and assets, on the new design system. Drop the Lenis scroll hijacking, custom cursor, WebGL fluid simulation, and full-viewport shaders — §30 is explicit that they harm LCP, the one metric a marketing page is judged on.
- Treat the claim audit as a gate, not a review.
