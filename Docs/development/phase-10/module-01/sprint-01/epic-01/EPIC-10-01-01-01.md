## 1. Epic Objective

Build the public site and resolve every inherited false claim.

---

## 2. Business Value

Appendix B exists because the existing site would, if shipped unchanged, make the company's central promise unbelievable the first time a user checked one of its claims.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** prospective user
> **I want** to understand what this does in fifteen seconds
> **So that** I can decide whether to trust it with my family history

> **As a** prospective user
> **I want** claims I can verify
> **So that** the trust the product asks for is earned rather than asserted

---
