## 1. Epic Objective

Build the flag system, execute the staged rollout, staff monitoring, and instrument the funnels.

---

## 2. Business Value

A launch you cannot observe or stop is a gamble. This epic makes it a controlled experiment.

---

## 3. User Stories

> **As a** team
> **I want** to stop the rollout in one action
> **So that** a surprise costs us dozens of users rather than thousands

> **As a** team
> **I want** to see whether people reach their first sealed capsule
> **So that** we know if the product actually works

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Flags with percentage and segment rollout
- Four-stage rollout with stop conditions
- Public status page
- On-call rota with the delivery alert routed
- Activation and retention funnels
- Cost ratios

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Flags in the database, evaluated server-side (ADR-025)
- PostHog first-party events with no PII
- Status page fed by real health checks
